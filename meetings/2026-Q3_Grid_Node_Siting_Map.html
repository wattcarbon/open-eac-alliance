How a location gets sited

Six real addresses, and how each one reaches an output. Pick an output to see the mechanism behind it. Energy, congestion and loss are priced at a grid node, so they share one search over the network. Capacity, ancillary services, T&D deferral and tariff savings come from whichever territory contains the address. The siting scores use neither: they are stored on the nearest substation, which borrows a congestion signal from the closest source in its market and takes its building stock from a 12 km catchment. The legend numbers the steps in the order they happen; select any step to read what it is, what selected it, and what it supplies.
